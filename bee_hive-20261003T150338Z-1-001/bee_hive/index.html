The Flow Hive invention

Launched in 2015, our revolutionary Flow Frame invention allows you to tap honey straight from the hive without opening it – it’s easier for the beekeeper and so much gentler on the bees.

You’ll no longer require expensive processing equipment, heavy lifting or hours of hot, sticky work to harvest. Simply sit back and watch as pure, fresh honey flows right out of the hive and into your jar. No mess, no fuss.

Our mission

More than beekeeping

At Flow, we’re about more than just creating beehives or harvesting honey. Our mission is to have a regenerative impact on the planet through innovation in beekeeping that inspires, empowers and connects.

As a certified B Corp, we’re always looking for a better way – from using materials that are as ethically and sustainably sourced to running our factory on 100% solar power – turning sunbeams into laserbeams to cut wooden panels for our Flow Hives.
